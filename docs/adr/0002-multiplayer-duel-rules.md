# Multi-player duels use Domain Format rules for free-for-all and official TCG Tag rules for 2v2

**Status:** accepted; updated 2026-10-07 by the product owner. Standard and Domain FFA3 and FFA4 use the [Domain Format rules](https://www.domainformat.com/rules) and the [TCGplayer Domain Format article](https://www.tcgplayer.com/content/article/How-To-Play-Domain-Yu-Gi-Oh-s-New-Multiplayer-Commander-Format/84ab787e-a192-4882-a721-3a71d57c650e/). Tag keeps the official TCG Tag rules. Every rule here has an outcome test (`docs/specs/multiplayer-rule-coverage.md` lists them); the known gaps are under "Open points for the engine test".

We are adding three multi-player formats next to the current 1v1 duel: **2v2 Tag**, **3-player free-for-all** and **4-player free-for-all**. All three work in both Standard and Domain duels. Tag, FFA3 and FFA4 allow only Master Rule 5. Master Rules 1 to 5 are available only for 1v1. The old-rule duel flags `OATH_OLD`, `ONCE_OLD_NEGATE` and `1_FACEUP_FIELD` stay refused at 3-4 seats. Each duelist has their own main field. In FFA4 and Tag, facing seats 0/1 and 2/3 share the Extra Monster Zones, Link arrows and mirrored columns, as specified below (owner decision 2026-10-07 for Tag). The EDOPro shared-field tag (one field and one LP total per team, partners swap each turn) was considered and rejected.

Konami publishes official rules only for Tag Duels ([TCG, revised 5 December 2019](https://www.yugioh-card.com/en/play/tagduel/)). The free-for-all rules below follow Domain Format, with the owner decisions of 2026-10-02. The first-draw rule below also applies to 1v1.

## Rules common to every multi-player format

- `[R-FFA-FIRST-DRAW]` **First draw by duel mode and Master Rule (all seat layouts).** Each duelist starts with 5 cards in hand. Tag, FFA3 and FFA4 use only Master Rule 5: in Domain every duelist draws on their first turn, including the turn-1 duelist; in Standard only the turn-1 duelist skips the draw. In 1v1, Master Rules 1 to 5 are allowed. In 1v1 Domain, the turn-1 duelist skips the draw on their first turn at every Master Rule; the second duelist draws on their first turn as usual. This follows the Domain Format Complete Rulebook v1.4, "Turn proceeding", and the owner's decision of 2026-10-04. Both the pinned and legacy 1v1 engines follow this rule; new 1v1 Domain duels save `firstTurnDraw: false`. Historical saved draw flags remain authoritative for recovery and replay. In 1v1 Standard MR1 and MR2, the turn-1 duelist draws, as specified by the stock core flags. In 1v1 Standard MR3, MR4 and MR5, only the turn-1 duelist skips the draw. MR3 removed the turn-1 draw on 21 March 2014. The duel creator shows: "In 1v1 Domain, the duelist who goes first skips the draw on their first turn at every Master Rule. The second duelist always draws." The rule id is kept for existing references.

- `[R-COMMON-SEP-FIELDS]` **Separate fields.** Each duelist has their own Main Monster Zones, Spell & Trap Zones, Field Zone, Pendulum Zones (per the Master Rule), Graveyard, banishment, hand, Deck and Extra Deck. In Domain, each duelist has their own Deck Master and Deck Master zone.
The FFA rules for "your opponent" are in the free-for-all section. Tag uses the opposing team under its official rules.
- `[R-COMMON-CONTROLLER-TURN]` **Your duelist's turn.** An effect or maintenance cost that applies during "your" Standby Phase, Battle Phase or End Phase uses the turn of the card's current controller. This is one real duelist in every format. In Tag, only that duelist's turn qualifies; the partner's turn is a separate turn. A maintenance cost paid with LP still uses the shared team LP under R-TAG-LP. A card that explicitly names its owner uses that owner as its text requires.
- `[R-COMMON-OPP-PICK]` **"Opponent" for hand, Deck, Extra Deck, draws and LP.** When an effect affects an opponent's hand, Deck or Extra Deck, makes an opponent draw, or targets an opponent's LP, the activating duelist **picks one opponent when they activate it**. The pick is part of the activation. Decision-only effects follow R-COMMON-OPP-DECISION instead. An informational hint does not ask for an opponent or bind one. An opponent hint goes to every living opponent; in Tag it does not go to the partner. In Tag, a "during your opponent's turn" condition does not select the opponent. For example, for Elfnote Tinia the activator picks the opposing member at activation; the turn player is not bound (owner, 2026-10-03 early morning).
- `[R-COMMON-CTRL]` **Damage to the controller.** When card text damages "the controller" of a card, or "you" as that card's controller, the duelist who controls that card when the effect resolves takes the damage. No other opponent takes this damage. In Tag, the controller's team LP takes the damage. The owner approved this rule on 2026-10-02.
- `[R-COMMON-OPP-DECISION]` **An opponent who only decides is chosen at resolution (owner approved 2026-10-04).** When an effect requires only an opponent's decision, without requiring that opponent's action or resources, "your opponent" becomes "an opponent". The activator does not declare that player at activation. During resolution, after preparing the choices, the activator picks one living opponent to decide, in FFA and Tag. A trigger's event opponent does not restrict this pick. Intimidating Ore - Summonite targets three own GY monsters at activation; at resolution the activator chooses one, then picks the opponent who chooses which summon effect to apply. Painful Choice selects five own Deck cards during resolution, then picks the opponent to see them and choose one. Eliminated seats are never offered; with one living opponent, that opponent decides without a seat prompt. This exception does not change effects requiring an opponent's action/resource or effects that target an opponent. Book of Eclipse keeps its separately approved activation declaration below.
- `[R-COMMON-ONGOING]` **Continuous face-up effects.** A continuous effect applies to all players or cards that meet its requirements. A continuous restriction on "your opponent" applies to all opponents, except Swords of Revealing Light in FFA (see R-FFA-SWORDS-PROTECT). A lasting lock created by an activated effect in FFA follows R-FFA-ACTIVATED-LOCK.
- `[R-COMMON-EACH-PLAYER]` **"Each player" / "both players" / "neither player".** "Each player" and "both players" mean every living duelist, including the Tag partner. "Neither player" means no player. For resource changes, each duelist acts in turn order, starting with the turn player. The Law of the Normal (66926224) can activate only if the activator has another card in hand and at least one opponent has a card in hand. At resolution, every living duelist discards their hand, including the Tag partner. An empty hand does nothing.
- `[R-COMMON-ALL-BOTH]` **"All" and both sides of the field.** An effect that affects "all" cards, or cards on both sides of the field (for example Dark Hole: "destroy all monsters on the field"), affects every duelist, you and your 2v2 partner included. An effect on "your opponent" affects only opponents, never your partner.
- `[R-COMMON-EMZ]` **Separate Extra Monster Zones (FFA3).** Each duelist has their own two Extra Monster Zones (EMZ). FFA3 columns use R-FFA-THREE-COLUMNS; this does not share its EMZ or Link arrows. Under Master Rule 5, as in 1v1, a duelist may use one EMZ, and may use the second only when the 1v1 rules allow it. Different duelists' EMZ do not block each other. FFA4 uses R-FFA-ACROSS-EMZ and Tag uses R-TAG-FACING: facing pairs 0/1 and 2/3 share EMZ, Link arrows and mirrored columns (owner decisions 2026-10-04 and 2026-10-07).
- `[R-COMMON-FL-LIST]` The Forbidden & Limited List applies per duelist (per Deck).
- `[R-COMMON-SEAT-STATE]` **Per-player flags and counters (owner decision Q6).** A flag or counter that a card keeps for each player (for example Curse of the Circle, Wiseman's Chalice, Fatal Abacus) has one slot per seat in free-for-all and one slot per team in Tag. The key is the team in Tag and the seat in free-for-all (rule R2 of the triage).
- `[R-COMMON-RETURN-TO-OWNER]` **Effects that return a card to its owner.** In every duel format, including 2v2 Tag, an effect that returns a card to its owner's field, hand, Deck, Extra Deck, Graveyard or banishment uses the real owner seat. The controller and the player who applies the effect do not change that destination. Apply the card text for the destination and the fallback when the return is not possible. This rule does not replace `[R-FFA-RETURN-OWNED-CARDS]`, which applies only to elimination. A control effect gives the card to the seat that the effect names. In FFA, 'your opponent' means the bound opponent of that effect, and 'you' means the effect's controller. When the effect ends, the remaining control effects decide the controller; with none, the card returns to its owner. An effect that returns a card to its owner gives it to the real owner's seat (R-COMMON-RETURN-TO-OWNER). If that owner is eliminated, the card stays with its controller; only tokens can be in this state (R-FFA-ELIMINATION). 1v1 and Tag keep the stock rules.

- `[R-COMMON-SURRENDER-EOT]` **Immediate surrender (owner rules, 2026-10-03 and 2026-10-04).** The id name is historical; the rule is immediate. Keep this id for existing test and coverage references. FFA3 and FFA4 surrender takes effect immediately on any player's turn. There is no surrender queue and no wait for the End Phase or turn end. With no open chain, the loss lands when the surrender is accepted. The leaver's owned cards leave the game, except Tokens; cards owned by living players return under R-FFA-RETURN-OWNED-CARDS. Future turns skip that seat. The next prompt of a living duelist cannot precede the loss. A living duelist's current choice stays open, including optional responses to events involving the leaver.

Return cleanup keeps the leaver's automatic answer and a living duelist's submitted answer, with the prompt's selection inputs, until the suspended processor resumes. If cleanup opens a required choice for a living duelist, that choice is answered first, then the saved answer resumes.

Engine decision under R-COMMON-SURRENDER-EOT: if surrender changes a counter offer, the engine asks again with the current sources when they can pay the full count, otherwise an effect removal returns false without removing counters or raising its event, and a REASON_COST removal stops the suspended cost script and cancels its pending summon before placement, using the core's failed-summon cleanup without refunding earlier completed payments (owner may override).

An unfinished activation or summon of the leaver is cancelled if no Chain Link is open. Its removed card cannot return to the board. The engine consumes the leaver's open choice with its automatic answer so that the cancelled operation can stop. A living duelist's opponent pick keeps its option ids, but an option for a removed seat is no longer legal when a living option remains.

If a cost prompt is already open when the player leaves, the cost finishes with the host's automatic answer, then the pending target and operation are cancelled.

Choices whose card source leaves the game are pruned. The living duelist keeps the remaining legal choices and normal response windows for that event.

Owner decision **2026-10-04** replaces the earlier FFA surrender exception. Standard and Domain FFA3 and FFA4 follow Domain Format Complete Rulebook v1.4, "Removing players from the game". All unresolved Chain Links of the removed player are removed without resolving. They raise no Lua chain-solving or chain-solved events and do not count as resolved. If the core already sent CHAIN_SOLVING before removal, it sends CHAIN_SOLVED to balance the host message state. GetChainInfo can read the stored fields of a removed link; negation and other writes stay blocked. Links of living players still resolve. This applies to LP loss, deck-out, surrender and disconnect timeout. The host answers a leaver's suspended choices deterministically and preserves living choices. A link whose operation already started stays visible and finishes that operation before the next link is checked. Tag and 1v1 keep their existing surrender rules.

If the leaver was the turn player and the duel continues, that turn goes through its remaining phases as if the leaver only passes priority and moves to the next phase. Battle Phase goes to Main Phase 2, then End Phase. Living duelists can still activate effects in these phases. After End Phase, the next living seat starts its turn, including Draw Phase. The leaver's later turns are skipped. Phase and turn-count resets still apply at their normal boundaries (owner decision, 2026-10-04; Domain Format Complete Rulebook v1.4). A living duelist's optional and required choices stay open with the same prompt and option ids, including responses to events involving the leaver. The current phase waits until those choices and any resulting chain finish. Living duelists receive their normal optional windows in every remaining phase. R-FFA-NO-ATTACK still applies. The last living duelist wins when all other living duelists surrender. Losses flagged together during a chain use the core's normal loss processing.

Tag surrender with no open chain loses the whole team immediately. Tag surrender with an open chain ends the duel at once, without resolving that chain. 1v1 surrender still ends the duel at once. All formats use "Surrender" as the result reason and in new elimination logs. Old saved "Surrendered" results stay readable. Time-limit losses keep their existing timing and fallback.

After a loss lands while an FFA duel continues, the player can explicitly ask to spectate (owner decision, 2026-10-04). Without that request, the host keeps the player role and own seat. A living or Leaving seat cannot spectate and receives HTTP 409. A spectator view hides private cards and sets mySide and prompt to null. After the duel completes, every seated player sees their own result, role and seat, including players in earlier loss groups and players who spectated during the duel. This also applies when the request asks to spectate. An interrupted duel keeps the same own-player flow. FFA draw seats stay eliminated and keep the player role. Tag draws have no seat-loss message. Room membership and seat records stay for results and replay. If an interrupted duel has no final board, a recorded loss command or saved surrendered seat for that seat (or its Tag team) permits a replay check for a loss that landed. A chain loss that did not land keeps the player role. If replay is unavailable and there is no final board, the room stays available with the player role. An explicit replay request still returns its error.

The journal records each accepted loss command as eliminate:0 at its revision. Recovery and replay apply that command at the same point and repeat the same internal automatic answers. Retired eliminate-eot commands are identified and refused; this engine cannot reproduce their old turn-end timing. The saved final board remains available.

New multiplayer surrender requires Debug.SurrenderDuelist and Debug.EliminateDuelist. The surrender function checks pending surrenders immediately without settling unfinished LP, Deck or effect-win losses, or advancing a pending time-limit loss. It removes FFA seats immediately and cancels their unresolved links, including while a chain is open. A core with only the old flag function cannot meet the immediate rule while another duelist holds a prompt. The host returns HTTP 409 for an unsupported core, saves no loss command, and starts no automatic answers. Install the immediate-surrender core before retrying. This requirement does not apply to 1v1 surrender or Tag surrender with an open chain, which the host ends directly.

## 2v2 Tag (official TCG Tag Duel rules)

- `[R-TAG-LP]` Two teams of two duelists. **Each team shares one LP total**, equal to the sum of its members' starting LP (16,000 with the default 8,000).
- `[R-TAG-TEAM-DAMAGE]` An effect that says "you take no battle damage", or otherwise prevents or reduces battle damage to "you", protects the whole team in Tag because the team shares one LP total (owner decision, 2026-10-06). In Tag, a condition about battle damage to "you" is also met by battle damage to the partner. Keep all other activation conditions, costs and effect limits.
- `[R-TAG-ORDER]` Seats are **p0=1A, p1=2A, p2=1B, p3=2B**. Team 1 (bottom) is p0+p2; team 2 (top) is p1+p3. Turn order is 1A, 2A, 1B, 2B. First draws follow R-FFA-FIRST-DRAW. The first three duelists cannot attack; the first Battle Phase is turn 4.
- `[R-TAG-SHARED-CARDS]` "You control", "your field" and "your Graveyard" include your partner's cards. "Your hand" and "your Deck" mean only your own.
- `[R-TAG-UNIQUE]` **Unique cards.** In Tag, a unique-on-field limit ("You can only control 1") permits one copy per team. Both team fields count.
- `[R-TAG-FACING]` **Facing geometry (owner decision 2026-10-07).** 1A faces 2A (p0/p1), and 1B faces 2B (p2/p3). Each pair shares its two EMZ, Link arrows and columns, with 1v1 mirroring: EMZ sequence 5 corresponds to the facing seat's 6 and vice versa; MMZ/STZ column 0 corresponds to 4. Occupied or disabled shared EMZ affect both facing players. Column groups, masks, summon conditions and Link queries see only the controller's field and its facing opponent, never its partner or the non-facing opponent. Reuse R-FFA-ACROSS-EMZ geometry; this does not change partner materials under R-TAG-PARTNER-COST. Sources: Domain Format Complete Rulebook v1.4, "Player positioning", and the owner's explicit Tag decision of 2026-10-07.
- `[R-TAG-FIELD]` **One Field Spell per team (owner decision 2026-10-07).** Before a new Field Spell is Set, activated from hand or placed by an effect in either member's Field Zone, send any old Field Spell controlled by either team member to its owner's Graveyard by rule, as in MR5 own-field replacement. Face-down cards also count. The opposing team's Field Spell stays. Each member still has its own Field Zone and controls its own placed card. Source: [Konami Tag rules, Basic Rules](https://www.yugioh-card.com/en/play/tagduel/).
- `[R-TAG-ATTACK]` **Direct attacks (owner decision 2026-10-07).** An ordinary direct attack is allowed only when **both opposing duelists** control no monsters in their MMZ or EMZ. A monster of either opponent guards the shared team LP. Both opponents' monsters remain legal attack targets, including EMZ; the attacking duelist cannot use its partner's monsters to attack. Card effects that explicitly allow direct attacks keep their normal exceptions. This supersedes the earlier per-member direct-attack exception. Source: [Konami Tag rules, Attacking](https://www.yugioh-card.com/en/play/tagduel/).
- `[R-TAG-PARTNER-COST]` You may use your partner's cards for costs, Tributes, materials and summon conditions. You cannot activate your partner's cards or effects.
- `[R-TAG-PARTNER]` Your partner is not your opponent. Your effects on "your opponent" do not affect your partner, and a card that works only on an opponent's card (for example Effect Veiler or Infinite Impermanence) cannot be used on your partner's card. A card that negates an activation, an effect or a summon (for example Solemn Judgment, Solemn Warning or Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring) cannot negate your partner's activation, effect or summon. It can negate the activations and summons of the opposing team, and your own, as in 1v1.
- `[R-TAG-VISIBILITY]` Partners may see each other's hands and Set cards.
- `[R-TAG-RESPONSE]` After a Chain Link, the opposing team gets the first chance to respond. Simultaneous triggers resolve in the order turn player, turn player's partner, then the opposing team.
- `[R-TAG-LOSS]` A team loses when its LP reaches 0, or when either of its duelists must draw from an empty Deck.
- `[R-TAG-TURN-COUNT]` Turn-count effects (for example Final Countdown) count every duelist's turn.

## 3-player and 4-player free-for-all (Domain Format rules)

These are the defaults. The organizer may later get options for LP.

- `[R-FFA-LP]` Each duelist has their own LP (default 8,000).
- `[R-FFA-ORDER]` Turn order is clockwise in seat order. First draws follow R-FFA-FIRST-DRAW.
- `[R-FFA-NO-ATTACK]` **The last duelist gets the first Battle Phase on their first turn.** In FFA3 this is turn 3; in FFA4 this is turn 4. Turns 1 through n-1 have no Battle Phase. Only living duelists count: a duelist eliminated before their first turn does not keep this window open. The window ends in the turn when the last living duelist starts their first turn.
- `[R-FFA-OPP-ONE]` **"Your opponent": one declared opponent.** A condition that does not change resources needs only one opponent to meet it. For an activated or ignition effect that affects an opponent's resources or field, declare ONE opponent at activation. Decision-only effects instead pick a living deciding opponent at resolution under R-COMMON-OPP-DECISION. Pick the opponent first, then any cards. The effect applies only to that opponent, including their field, Graveyard and banishment. Raigeki and Harpie's Feather Duster affect one declared opponent. Book of Eclipse and Prediction Princess Astromorrigan also use one declared opponent; their later End Phase effects affect only that opponent. An effect with "either GY" text, such as Monster Reborn, declares no opponent; its target choice is a card choice. It can target a monster in the Graveyard of any living duelist.
- `[R-FFA-OPP-RESPONSE]` **Trigger and response effects.** An effect on "your opponent" applies only to the opponent or opponents that met its requirement. A Mirror Force-type response to an opponent's monster declaring an attack is open to every duelist for whom the attacker is an opponent. If A attacks B, C may activate Mirror Force, but it affects only A's monsters. A direct-attack response, such as Battle Fader, Gorz, Drowning Mirror Force or Number 100: Numeron Dragon, is offered only to the attacked duelist (in Tag, the attacked team). Abyss Actor - Liberty Dramatist goes to the field of the opponent whose monster or LP the declared attack targets (owner, 2026-10-03 early morning). In FFA, a phase trigger that names no opponent's turn declares one opponent that meets its condition. A trigger in "your opponent's <phase>" binds the turn player with no pick. If the saved causal opponent leaves and an availability check fails, the queued link stays on the chain. It uses its count limit and completes with no effect. No cost is paid when the failed check stops the cost callback. Other players may respond to the link. This is an engine decision; owner can change it.
- `[R-FFA-SWORDS-PROTECT]` **Swords of Revealing Light is legal in FFA.** Face-up Swords protects only its controller and that controller's monsters from attacks. Other opponents can attack each other. It flips every opponent's face-down monsters. It leaves the field after three living opponent turns under stock R3. This exception applies only to Swords. Tag and 1v1 keep the stock card rule. If a defender is eliminated before a redirect to a direct attack resolves in FFA, the fallback uses ascending seat order. It selects the lowest living opposing seat that permits a direct attack and skips seats protected by Swords. A living defender remains the defender; if Swords protects that seat, the redirect fails.
- `[R-FFA-ACTIVATED-LOCK]` **Lasting locks from activated cards.** A lock that an activated card creates on "your opponent", such as "your opponent cannot ... this turn", applies to ONE opponent declared at activation. Face-up continuous effects still follow R-COMMON-ONGOING.
- `[R-FFA-DECLARED-DURATION]` **Declared-opponent duration (owner decisions, 2026-10-03 and 2026-10-04).** In FFA, this rule applies only to player effects (owner, 2026-10-02 late). A player effect is an effect registered on a player with `Duel.RegisterEffect` (`EFFECT_FLAG_FIELD_ONLY`). After a player effect binds ONE declared opponent, a duration such as "until your opponent's next turn" (`RESET_OPPO_TURN` without `RESET_SELF_TURN`) counts only that opponent's turns. Time Seal skips that opponent's next Draw Phase. Appointer of the Red Lotus returns the card during that opponent's next End Phase. SINGLE, EQUIP and FIELD effects registered on a card keep R3, even when they bind one declared opponent. Swords of Revealing Light keeps R3: each opponent's turn counts (owner, 2026-10-02 night). Player effects with no declared opponent also keep R3 below. If the declared seat is removed, the effect keeps the same duration. Its skipped phase boundaries still count for expiry; they create no actual turn, activation, draw or response window. The owner decision on 2026-10-04 replaces the eliminated-seat R3 fallback approved on 2026-10-02. 1v1 and Tag do not change.
- `[R-FFA-LOCK-CONTROL-CHANGE]` **A declared opponent takes the source of a continuous lock.** In FFA, only the source card's own continuous locks, active while it is face-up, apply to the old controller (the seat that lost the card) when the declared opponent takes control of the source card. A lasting lock already registered on a player stays on that player, as in 1v1. Other FFA opponents stay free. Tag keeps the opposing-team rule. The owner answered on 2026-10-02 evening (rule) and 2026-10-03 (scope).
- `[R-FFA-RESOURCE-ROTATION]` **Resource changes between duelists.** Each living duelist acts in turn order, starting with the turn player. For Creature Swap-type effects, every living duelist must control a monster and chooses one. Each chosen monster goes to the next living duelist in turn order; each duelist receives the monster of the previous living duelist. The moves form one rotation, including when all Main Monster Zones are full. If a player leaves during the rotation, including by timeout or not returning on their turn, the whole swap, including the card choices, all placements and the rotation, finishes before that player is removed: their cards leave the game and cards owned by other players return to their owners (owner decision, 2026-10-05).
- `[R-FFA-ACROSS-EMZ]` **Shared Extra Monster Zones and columns in FFA4.** Owner decision 2026-10-04: the facing pairs are 0/1 and 2/3 (`partner = seat ^ 1`), replacing 0/2 and 1/3. In the 2x2 Domain Format stream layout, seat 0 bottom-left faces seat 1 top-left, and seat 2 top-right faces seat 3 bottom-right. Each pair shares two EMZ and columns as in 1v1. A monster in one EMZ blocks the matching EMZ of its partner, mirrored as in 1v1. Link arrows and column effects use that partner; an eliminated partner contributes no columns or shared zones. Turn order stays 0, 1, 2, 3 clockwise. FFA3 keeps separate EMZ and Link arrows; its columns use R-FFA-THREE-COLUMNS. Tag and 1v1 keep their existing rules.
- `[R-FFA-THREE-COLUMNS]` **FFA3 column peer (owner, 2026-10-05; house rule).** With three living duelists, an activated column effect that affects opponent cards declares one living opponent under R-FFA-OPP-ONE before any card or zone choice. Only that opponent's columns line up: own Main Zone `s` faces opponent Main Zone `4-s`; EMZ sequences 5 and 6 are in columns 1 and 3. The existing causal-opponent rule still applies to responses. Non-activated column logic (summon conditions, continuous effects and the lasting part of Infinite Impermanence) uses only the own field. With two living duelists, every column query uses the remaining opponent, without an opponent prompt, for all surviving seat pairs (0/1, 0/2, 1/2). An eliminated seat is never a peer or a choice. This changes only columns: FFA3 EMZ remain independent, including independently occupied mirrored EMZ in one column, and Link arrows do not share. FFA4 keeps its fixed living facing pair and never re-faces; Tag and 1v1 stay unchanged. See [card scope and UI handoff](../specs/ffa3-columns.md).
- `[R-FFA-CHAIN]` **Chain responses (owner, 2026-10-04; Domain Format Complete Rulebook v1.4, "Turn proceeding").** After a duelist adds a Chain Link, the next living duelist in clockwise turn order gets the first chance to respond. Responses continue clockwise, with the activating duelist last. Each new link starts a fresh pass round after its activating duelist. The chain resolves when every living duelist passes in a row after the last link. Eliminated and leaving seats are skipped. Example: A activates, B passes, C chains; D responds first, then A, B and C. This applies to FFA3 and FFA4, in Standard and Domain. Open game state, SEGOC and simultaneous trigger ordering keep their existing rules. After a SEGOC trigger chain or a mandatory quick-trigger link is added, the first response goes clockwise to the next living seat after the last link's controller, even when that controller is not the turn player.
  - Example (4 players, player 1 is the turn player): player 1 activates a card. Players 2, 3, 4 and then 1 may respond. Player 2 chains. Player 3 responds first, then players 4, 1 and 2.
  - **Cost elimination (owner, 2026-10-06):** if paying a cost eliminates the activator and removes the last living link, clean up the chain and give exactly one normal open-state round, starting with the turn player (or the next living seat if that player left), then clockwise. Do not offer an extra round before `CHAIN_END`. If living links remain, restart responses after the newest living link's controller. A queued responder must be chosen again after the cost; an already displayed living player's choice stays open. Native proof: `cost-elimination-response` (FFA3, FFA4, surviving lower links, eliminated turn player and Tag control); checker proof: `nduel-response-order`.
- `[R-FFA-TRIGGERS]` **Simultaneous triggers** go on the chain in turn order: the turn player first, then clockwise.
- `[R-FFA-NEGATE]` A card that negates an activation, an effect or a summon (for example Solemn Judgment or Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring) can negate the activation, effect or summon of any duelist, as in 1v1.
- `[R-FFA-ATTACK]` The attacking duelist picks any opponent's monster, or makes a direct attack on an opponent who controls no monster.
- `[R-FFA-ELIMINATION]` A duelist loses at 0 LP, deck-out, surrender or disconnect timeout. Their cards leave the game, including cards they own on other fields; their Tokens on living fields stay. Seats do not move. **All unresolved Chain Links of that player are removed without resolving**, including surrender links (owner decision, 2026-10-04; Domain Format Complete Rulebook v1.4). They do not raise resolution events. Other players' links still resolve.
- `[R-FFA-RETURN-OWNED-CARDS]` **Return cards owned by other duelists after elimination.** A card the loser controls but another duelist owns returns to the owner's zone of the same kind, with its position kept where applicable. Cards in the loser's hand, Graveyard or banishment return to the owner's same location. If a card cannot return to that zone, it goes to the owner's Graveyard. This return cannot be responded to: the movement opens no response window and raises no activation event, including for a Sangan sent to the Graveyard. Continuous destination rules still apply (for example Pendulums and Macro Cosmos). Owner decision, 2026-10-04; Domain Format Complete Rulebook v1.4. Equip cards owned by living players that lose their target are destroyed by REASON_RULE and REASON_LOST_TARGET, with their normal destruction events.
- **Resolved effects keep their full duration after removal.** Eradicator Epidemic Virus still applies to its victim until the end of that victim's third turn. Removing the source does not cancel a lasting effect that already resolved. A duration that waits for the removed player's turn or next Draw Phase expires at the same place in the seat order as if that player were still playing. Heat Wave lasts through the other seats' turns and expires before the next living turn at the place where its activator's next Draw Phase would start. Future removed-seat turns create no phase events or activation windows; only their expiry boundaries advance. Face-up continuous effects still need their source in play. Owner decision, 2026-10-04; Domain Format Complete Rulebook v1.4.
- **A removed turn player passes through the remaining phases.** Living duelists keep their normal activation opportunities. The End Phase counts for every normal turn count (for example `RESET_OPPO_TURN` and Nightmare's Steelcage). Only later turns of the removed seat are skipped.
- `[R-FFA-WINNER]` The last duelist left wins.
  - If the last duelists left reach 0 LP at the same time (for example one effect that damages all of them), nobody is left: the duel is a **draw** and has no winner (`winnerSeat` is null). If one effect takes all but one duelist to 0 LP, the one duelist left wins.

### Answers to the ten triage questions (2026-10-01, product owner)

The triage of 457 cards that needed a product decision is in `.status/multiplayer-triage.md` (a local note, git-ignored). The owner answered its ten questions. Questions 2 and 5 are answered with a different rule than the one proposed there.

1. **Turn count (Q1): option (a), rule R3.** In FFA, effects that do not follow R-FFA-DECLARED-DURATION count every turn of any living opponent as one opponent turn. Only player effects registered with `Duel.RegisterEffect` (`EFFECT_FLAG_FIELD_ONLY`) and bound to one declared opponent follow R-FFA-DECLARED-DURATION in the FFA rule list. SINGLE, EQUIP and FIELD effects registered on a card keep R3. Player effects use R3 if no opponent was declared. The 2026-10-04 decision replaces the removed-opponent fallback: a player effect keeps its declared duration and expires at the same skipped-seat boundary. In Tag, only a turn of the two opposing duelists counts. About 30 cards use `RESET_OPPO_TURN` with a count of 2 or more. Effects that keep R3 get shorter in rounds (N/2 rounds with 3 players and in Tag, N/3 rounds with 4 players). The review `.status/multiplayer-turncount-review.md` (a local note, git-ignored) rated all 30 cards and 24 more cards with an own counter: **no new bans**. Some cards are on a WATCH list (stronger but fair, for example Double Payback, Magical Spring, Memory of an Adversary, Steel Scorpion, Extinction on Schedule, Clock Tower Prison, Life Shaver, Vain Betrayer). They stay legal. **The 4 free-for-all turn-count bans stay**: Doom Virus Dragon, The Wicked Avatar, Grisaille Prison and Million-Century Ice Prison. Destiny Board is already forbidden (alternative win). R3 is not the same as `[R-TAG-TURN-COUNT]`, which counts every duelist turn, own team included.
   **Swords of Revealing Light is legal in FFA.** It follows R-FFA-SWORDS-PROTECT below. The four other turn-count bans remain.
2. **Count and compare cards (Q2): changed meaning.** "Your opponent controls more ..." and the cards that compare field, hand or card counts (about 50, for example Evenly Matched and Pineapple Blast) do **not** add all opponents together. In free-for-all, the activator **picks ONE opponent** when they activate the card (bind), and the card compares with that opponent only. In Tag, the card compares with the **combined** field or hand of the two opposing duelists (the opposing team). This replaces the proposal "add all opponents together". In FFA, the 2026-10-02 R-FFA-OPP-ONE rule also applies to other activated effects on "your opponent". In FFA, a compare that is only a condition and changes no resource declares no opponent (for example Re-Cover). One opponent that meets the condition is enough (owner, 2026-10-02).
3. **"Each player" (Q3): accepted.** "Each player" is every living duelist, the Tag partner included (rule R1, `[R-COMMON-EACH-PLAYER]`).
4. **Duel-style cards (Q4): accepted.** Ante, Dragged Down, Dice Jar, Reversal Quiz, Trading Places and the other cards of this kind (about 80) act on you and one picked opponent. The other opponents do nothing. Tag uses the team LP.
5. **"Your opponent chooses" (Q5): changed meaning.** When the card text says "your opponent" in the singular, **ONE opponent chooses**: the picked or bound opponent, in free-for-all and in Tag. Only when the card text says "all" or "each" opponent do all of them choose. This replaces rule R4 of the triage ("each affected opponent chooses from their own cards"). Decision-only effects now pick that opponent during resolution under R-COMMON-OPP-DECISION (rulebook v1.4, approved 2026-10-04). Effects that require an opponent's action or resources keep their activation declaration or causal opponent. `Select*(1-tp)` makes the opponent bound at the appropriate stage choose.
6. **Per-player flags and counters (Q6): accepted.** The key is the team in Tag and the seat in free-for-all (rule R2).
7. **Swap of control (Q7): accepted.** The activator may pick a monster of any opponent. A targeted swap is between one of your own monsters and that monster. Creature Swap-type effects in FFA now use R-FFA-RESOURCE-ROTATION (2026-10-02). Mirror Gate needs a script fix (see question 10).
8. **Tribute of an opponent monster (Q8): accepted, with more.** The "Tribute a monster your opponent controls" effects (`EXTRA_RELEASE`, rule R5) work for the monsters of any opponent. This is a core fix: the engine found only the monsters of seat 1 before. It includes the Kaiju, Lava Golem and **The Winged Dragon of Ra - Sphere Mode**. For Ra Sphere Mode, all Tributed monsters come from ONE opponent, and the monster goes to the field of that opponent. Ra Sphere Mode therefore follows the Kaiju and Lava Golem rule (the Kaiju bullet above). It does not follow the rule "the summoning player picks one opponent". The earlier text put it in that group and said it Tributes your own monsters. That was wrong.
9. **Defeated duelists (Q9): accepted.** "Each player" effects skip a duelist who has lost, as `[R-FFA-ELIMINATION]` says.
10. **New bans (Q10): none.** The owner said "make those fixes now". Five cards get a per-card script fix: Contract with Don Thousand, Tsumuha-Kutsunagi the Lord of Swords, Infernoid Tierra, Two-for-One Team and Mirror Gate (it needs a check that the attacked monster belongs to the activating duelist). Two cards get a label fix, because the label must keep the real seat: Curse of the Circle and Wiseman's Chalice. The 4 turn-count bans above are not new.

These cards stay legal in the deck check. Their rules are listed in `MULTIPLAYER_CARD_RULES` (`packages/duel-server/src/banlists/multiplayer.ts`). `engine: "native"` means that the engine does the rule and a live scenario proves it for that card (the proven tables are in `proven`). `engine: "pending"` means that no live scenario proves it for that card yet. ## Domain deck rules (2026-10-02)

Domain uses exactly 60 Main Deck cards and at most 15 Extra Deck cards. Main and Extra are singleton by card identity, including alternate art and treated-as names. The Deck Master is separate from these counts and cannot also be in the decks. There is no Side Deck and no banlist by default; the host may choose a banlist, and the multiplayer safety list still applies.

The Domain includes the Deck Master's archetypes, Attributes and Monster Types, and those mentioned in its effect text. Attribute and Type mentions match complete words, including plural Types, compound Types and token definitions; substrings such as "darkness" or "rocket" do not count. Quoted Attribute or Type labels count, while words inside quoted card or archetype names do not add Attributes or Types. A specifically named card is allowed, and its archetypes are included. A Normal Pendulum Deck Master's Pendulum Effect box counts; its separate Flavor Text (or Monster Effect) box does not. Other Normal Monster flavor text does not add Domain membership. Divine Attribute and Divine-Beast are in every Domain. Spell and Trap Cards have no Domain restriction.

## Deck Master core rules (owner approved 2026-10-04)

The cumulative DMZ leave cost is 500 LP per completed return and the first leave is free. This pays for the action, including activating a Main Deck Pendulum Deck Master as a Pendulum Spell. It carries no spell activation effect as its LP-cost reason, so Spell Economics cannot waive it. Ordinary card costs, including Chain Energy on a monster summoned from hand, still apply. These rules apply to the pinned 1v1, multiplayer Domain and legacy 1v1 Domain cores.

Returning a Deck Master to the DMZ is a rule move, with no effect or reason player. It raises both single-card and grouped `EVENT_MOVE` events with the previous location, so triggers for leaving banishment, hand, Deck or Extra Deck can start a new chain after the return. Leaving the GY additionally raises `EVENT_LEAVE_GRAVE`, including the Exosister interaction. The return itself does not start a chain.

A Deck Master in the DMZ cannot be targeted or affected by other cards, including effects flagged to ignore immunity. Only summon procedures owned by and registered on the Deck Master are exempt from the effect gate. Effects granted by other cards remain blocked after recall, including single effects that normally bypass immunity checks. Normal/Tribute Summons, proper mechanic summons, and permitted Main Deck Pendulum leaves remain available; a rule move with no effect reason also remains valid.

Domain 1v1 accepts Master Rules 1–5; multiplayer accepts MR5. A non-Link Extra Deck Deck Master summoned from the DMZ can use any available Main Monster Zone and, in MR4/5, either Extra Monster Zone. This exception does not depend on the MR5 Fusion/Synchro/Xyz placement flag. A Link Deck Master uses an Extra Monster Zone or a Main Monster Zone a Link Monster points to; MR1–3 have no Extra Monster Zones, so it needs a linked Main Monster Zone. Material checks recompute arrows after removing the selected materials. Ordinary Extra Deck monsters retain their Master Rule placement restrictions.
